## About us
We are a boutique jewelry studio specializing in fine and custom pieces. We
showcase our work on Instagram and help customers choose or commission pieces,
then book a consultation with our owner/specialist.

## Location & hours
- Studio: 123 Example Street, Your City (by appointment).
- Hours: Tue–Sat, 10am–6pm. Closed Sun/Mon.
- We serve both walk-in (by appointment) and virtual consultations.

## What we make
Engagement rings, wedding bands, necklaces, earrings, bracelets, and fully
custom/bespoke commissions. We work in 14K/18K gold (yellow, white, rose) and
platinum, with natural and lab-grown diamonds and colored gemstones.

## Pricing
- Prices for in-stock pieces are fixed and are provided by our assistant when
  you tell us which piece you're interested in.
- Custom/bespoke pieces are quoted after a consultation, because the price
  depends on metal, stones, and design.

## Custom / bespoke process
1. Share your idea or a reference image via DM.
2. Book a consultation with our specialist.
3. We provide a design and a precise quote.
4. Production typically takes 3–6 weeks depending on complexity.

## Authenticity & certification
- Diamonds above 0.5ct come with a grading report (e.g., IGI/GIA).
- Gold is hallmarked for purity.
- We provide a certificate of authenticity with every fine piece.

## Sizing & resizing
- Most rings are resizable (US 4–9). Tell us your size at booking.
- One complimentary resize within 30 days of purchase.

## Care & warranty
- 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects.
- Complimentary cleaning and inspection any time.
- Care guidance provided with each piece.

## Shipping & returns
- Insured shipping available domestically and internationally (quoted per order).
- Returns/exchanges within 14 days for in-stock, non-custom items in original condition.
- Custom pieces are non-refundable once production begins.

## Payment
- Payment and deposits are handled directly with our specialist during or after
  the consultation. We do not take payment through Instagram.

## Booking a consultation
- We offer in-store, video, and phone consultations.
- Our assistant can share a booking link so you can pick a time that suits you.
